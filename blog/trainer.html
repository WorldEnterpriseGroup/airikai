200 Hour Certification course

Embark on your journey to become a certified Airikai Sacred Warrior Trainer with our comprehensive 200-hour certification program. This intensive training course is designed to equip you with the knowledge, skills, and spiritual foundation needed to guide others on their sacred warrior path.

What You'll Learn

  • Foundational Philosophy: Deep understanding of sacred warrior principles and ancient wisdom traditions
  • Movement Techniques: Comprehensive training in martial arts, yoga, and movement practices
  • Teaching Methodology: Effective instruction techniques and class management
  • Energy Work: Understanding and working with subtle energies and chakra systems
  • Spiritual Development: Personal growth and spiritual practice guidance

Course Structure

The 200-hour certification is divided into several modules:

Module 1: Foundation (50 hours)

Core principles, history, and philosophy of the sacred warrior path.

Module 2: Movement (75 hours)

Physical training, martial arts forms, and body awareness practices.

Module 3: Teaching (50 hours)

Instruction methods, class planning, and student interaction.

Module 4: Integration (25 hours)

Personal practice, final assessments, and certification ceremony.

"A true teacher is one who has walked the path and can guide others with wisdom, compassion, and authentic understanding."

Airikai Training Manual

Certification Requirements

To receive your Trainer certification, you must:

  • Complete all 200 hours of training
  • Pass written and practical examinations
  • Demonstrate teaching competency
  • Maintain consistent personal practice
  • Embody the sacred warrior principles in daily life
