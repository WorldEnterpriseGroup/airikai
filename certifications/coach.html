Coach Certification

Transformational Coaching & Mentorship Program

Become a Transformational Coach

Become a coach in the Airikai Program. This program gives you all the tools to coach other teachers in pilates, yoga, tango, and digital design in a fun & creative way.

Program Overview

The Airikai Coach Certification develops your ability to mentor and guide other teachers in their professional and personal growth. This specialized program focuses on coaching methodologies, leadership development, and creating sustainable transformation in others.

Program Duration

6 months intensive coaching training
Monthly workshops + mentorship practicum

Training Locations

Los Angeles, California
Virtual coaching sessions included

Core Coaching Competencies

Coaching Psychology

Understanding motivation, behavior change, and human development

Communication Mastery

Active listening, powerful questioning, and feedback delivery

Goal Setting & Accountability

Creating clear objectives and supporting achievement

Teacher Development

Mentoring teaching skills and professional growth

Training Curriculum

Month 1-2 Coaching Foundations
  • Coaching philosophy and ethics
  • Adult learning principles
  • Emotional intelligence development
  • Creating safe coaching environments
Month 3-4 Skill Development
  • Advanced coaching conversations
  • Conflict resolution and difficult dialogues
  • Group coaching dynamics
  • Assessment and evaluation tools
Month 5 Practicum & Integration
  • Supervised coaching practice
  • Case study development
  • Feedback and reflection sessions
  • Business of coaching workshop
Month 6 Certification & Launch
  • Final coaching assessments
  • Portfolio presentation
  • Coaching practice setup
  • Coach certification ceremony

Investment & Requirements

Prerequisites
  • Airikai Trainer (200hr) or Expert Trainer (500hr) Certification
  • Minimum 1 year of teaching experience
  • Demonstrated leadership or mentoring experience
  • Personal coaching session with program director
  • Commitment to coaching others upon completion
What's Included
  • Comprehensive coaching methodology training
  • Coaching tools and resource library
  • Supervised coaching practice opportunities
  • Mentorship from certified coaches
  • Airikai Coach Certificate
  • Authorized to coach Airikai teachers
  • Access to coach community network
  • Continuing education in coaching skills
