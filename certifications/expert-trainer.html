Program Overview

The Airikai Expert Trainer Certification elevates your teaching to mastery level. This comprehensive 500-hour advanced program deepens your understanding of movement science, advanced teaching techniques, and leadership skills to guide transformational experiences.

Monthly intensives + 3 immersion weeks

Month 1-3 Advanced Foundation Phase

  • Advanced anatomy and physiology

Month 4-6 Specialty Training
  • Therapeutic and corrective exercise

Month 7-9 Leadership Development
  • Teacher training methodology

Month 10-12 Master Certification
  • Student teaching supervision

  • Professional development planning
