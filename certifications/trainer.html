Trainer Certification

200 Hour Foundational Teaching Program

Begin Your Teaching Journey

200 Hour Certification in the Airikai Program. This program gives you all the tools to teach pilates, yoga, tango, and digital design in a fun & creative way.

Program Overview

The Airikai Trainer Certification is your gateway to becoming a movement educator. This comprehensive 200-hour program provides the foundational knowledge and practical skills needed to guide others on their journey of transformation.

Program Duration

6 months intensive training
Weekends + 2 week immersion

Training Locations

Los Angeles, California
Online modules available

What You'll Master

Movement Foundations

Anatomy, biomechanics, and safe teaching practices

Teaching Methodology

Class structure, sequencing, and student assessment

Multi-Discipline Integration

Yoga, Pilates, martial arts, and dance fusion

Business Skills

Marketing, client relations, and studio management

Training Curriculum

Month 1-2 Foundation Phase
  • Airikai philosophy and principles
  • Basic anatomy and physiology
  • Fundamental movement patterns
  • Introduction to teaching
Month 3-4 Skill Development
  • Advanced movement techniques
  • Class planning and sequencing
  • Hands-on adjustments
  • Voice and presence training
Month 5 Integration Week
  • Intensive 7-day immersion
  • Master classes with senior teachers
  • Practice teaching sessions
  • Personal development workshops
Month 6 Certification Phase
  • Student teaching practicum
  • Final assessments
  • Business planning
  • Graduation ceremony

Investment & Requirements

Prerequisites
  • Minimum 1 year of consistent movement practice
  • Completion of Airikai fundamentals course or equivalent
  • Letter of recommendation from a movement teacher
  • Personal interview with program director
What's Included
  • 200 hours of instruction and practice
  • Comprehensive training manual
  • Access to online learning portal
  • Mentorship from certified instructors
  • Airikai Trainer Certificate upon completion
  • Listing in Airikai teacher directory
  • Continuing education opportunities
