Tantric Tango

Discover the Sacred Dance of Connection

Experience the Passion and Soul of Tango

A beautiful introduction to the history of Tango, the emotion, passion, and mood of this relationship between two.

Course Overview

Tantric Tango transcends traditional dance instruction by integrating the spiritual and emotional dimensions of partnership. This course explores the sacred connection between two souls through the sensual art of Tango:

  • Historical Context: Explore the rich cultural heritage and origins of Tango
  • Emotional Expression: Learn to communicate feelings through movement and embrace
  • Partner Connection: Develop intuitive communication without words
  • Sacred Intimacy: Understand the spiritual dimension of partnership dance

What You'll Learn

Tango Fundamentals

Master the basic steps, posture, and traditional techniques of Argentine Tango.

Emotional Intelligence

Develop sensitivity to your partner's energy and emotional state through dance.

Sacred Partnership

Learn to create sacred space and honor the divine in your dance partner.

Musical Interpretation

Express the passion and story within traditional Tango music through movement.

  • Improvisation and creative expression

  • Storytelling through Tango movement
  • Performance preparation and presentation
  • Incorporating Tantric principles into dance
  • Teaching others the sacred art of Tango
